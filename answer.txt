1. According to the text, what is a recommended prevention method for reducing the risk of developing metabolic forms of diabetes?
A recommended prevention method for reducing the risk of developing metabolic forms of diabetes, such as type 2 diabetes and gestational diabetes, is managing weight through regular exercise and a sensible diet. This practice helps the body use glucose effectively and prevent or reduce hyperglycemia. Additionally, controlling blood pressure and cholesterol, avoiding smoking, restricting alcohol, and maintaining a healthy lifestyle are also beneficial in reducing the risk of developing metabolic forms of diabetes.
A) Regular exercise and sensible diet
Regular exercise and a sensible diet are crucial aspects of managing diabetes, especially for individuals with type 2 diabetes and gestational diabetes. These lifestyle interventions help the body use glucose effectively, prevent or reduce hyperglycemia, and can also help individuals with other forms of diabetes avoid insulin resistance and double diabetes. It is essential to maintain a healthy weight through regular physical activity and a balanced diet to manage diabetes effectively.
B) Genetic testing
Genetic testing can help diagnose conditions such as maturity-onset diabetes of the young and Wolfram syndrome. It can reveal who is at risk for certain types of diabetes.
C) Insulin therapy
Patients with diabetes, especially type 1 diabetes, Wolfram syndrome, and eventually all people with latent autoimmune diabetes of adulthood, require regular insulin therapy to manage their condition. Insulin therapy is essential for these individuals to regulate their blood sugar levels effectively. There are various forms of insulin administration, including syringe injections, insulin pumps, insulin pens, jet injectors, and inhaled insulin. It is crucial for patients to work closely with their healthcare providers to determine the most suitable insulin regimen for their specific needs.
D) Smoking cessation
Smoking cessation is an important aspect of managing diabetes. Cigarette smoking is a risk factor for type 2 diabetes and possibly other forms of diabetes. It is recommended to quit smoking to reduce the risk of developing diabetes and to manage the condition effectively.

A) Ophthalmologist
Patients with diabetes need to see an ophthalmologist regularly as part of their care plan. Regular dilated eye exams are essential to monitor for any signs of diabetic retinopathy, a common complication of diabetes that can lead to blindness if left untreated. The ophthalmologist will check for any changes in the blood vessels in the retina and provide necessary treatment to prevent vision loss.

C) Endocrinologist

D) Podiatrist
A podiatrist is a healthcare professional who specializes in treating foot and ankle conditions. Patients with diabetes are often referred to podiatrists for regular foot examinations and care as part of their diabetes management plan.
